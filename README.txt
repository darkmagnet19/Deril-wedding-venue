The page and both venue links are included. The invitation artwork is used as the page background.

FREE HOSTING WITH NETLIFY DROP:
1. Extract this ZIP on your computer.
2. Open https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign in or create a free account.
3. Drag the extracted 'wedding_venue_site' folder onto the Netlify Drop page.
4. Netlify will publish it and give you a public URL ending in netlify.app.
5. Send that URL back here so the final QR code can be generated for the live page.

The QR must encode the public hosted URL; a QR made before hosting won't work for guests.
